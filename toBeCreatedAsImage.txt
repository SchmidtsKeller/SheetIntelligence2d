IMAGES STILL TO BE CREATED
==========================

Every texture or drawn shape in the game that a painted image will replace.
In the running game each of them says "Placeholder" (pink letters) until its
image exists. Remove an entry when its image is in the game.

Where the work is tracked:
  #30  Create the painted image assets (49 sprites, sheets and tiles):
       characters, creatures, environment, blood. Section D below points to
       its file names instead of repeating them.
  #70  Design and create the buttons and UI elements in the game's painted
       style: sections A, B and C (menus, HUD, touch controls). #30 leaves
       these out on purpose.
  #22  Art direction pass: wires the images into the scenes and the theme.

Style for all of them: resources/documents/art_style_constraints.md
(atmospheric realism painting, oil on canvas, heavy chiaroscuro, muted
earthy palette: raw umber, burnt sienna, olive, charcoal; sickly yellow or
cold white highlights; blood and danger in oxidized crimson). World sprites:
strict top-down view, facing the top edge, no baked-in shadows (the engine
lights them). UI images: the same materials and palette, but text and icons
must stay readable on a phone held sideways (contrast at least 4.5:1, touch
targets at least 48 px).

Format of an entry:
  <image id>  what it is
    used in:  file  ->  node (the id is also given in code, e.g. image_id)
    size:     pixels to paint at (aspect ratio), transparency
    style:    notes beyond the general style
    covered:  the issue and file name where it is planned

All UI ids are referenced in code: GameTheme (ui/theme/game_theme.gd) passes
them to PlaceholderStyleBox, the components pass them to PlaceholderBadge.

  used in:  ui/screens/lobby_screen.gd  ->  Scroll/.../LeftBoard/Left/Title
            ui/screens/boot_screen.tscn ->  Layout/Title
            (both via GameLabel.mark_as_logo, ui/components/game_label.gd)
  size:     1024x256 (4:1), transparent; shown about 440x76 px
  style:    painted, worn lettering in sickly yellow on nothing; may carry a
            hint of wool or a sheep's ear turning into a claw. Must read at
            300 px wide on a phone.
  covered:  #70

ui_backdrop  The picture behind every menu screen.
  used in:  ui/components/screen_backdrop.gd (ScreenBackdrop, node "Backdrop")
            in the lobby, the scoreboard, the boot screen and the pairing
            panel; style BackdropPanel in ui/theme/game_theme.gd
  size:     1920x1080 (16:9), opaque. The window keeps the height and shows
            more or less width ("expand"), so it must survive cropping to
            4:3 and widening to 21:9: keep the middle quiet, detail at the
            edges.
  style:    a pasture at night seen from above, almost black, a few sheep
            shapes and one faint lantern glow; very low contrast behind the
            boards so text on top stays readable.
  covered:  #70

ui_board  The board behind a group of texts and buttons.
  used in:  ui/components/game_panel.gd (GamePanel): LeftBoard, RightBoard
            (ui/lobby/two_columns.gd, lobby and scoreboard), Board (the
            pairing panel); style BoardPanel in ui/theme/game_theme.gd
  size:     256x256 nine-patch (StyleBoxTexture), 32 px margins, opaque or
            mostly opaque; stretches to 300..560 px wide and 200..700 px high.
            Content margins today: 22 px left/right, 16 px top/bottom.
  style:    stained parchment nailed to weathered wood, or a dark wooden
            board with iron corners; the middle must be calm and even.
  covered:  #70

ui_button  The menu button, in six states.
  used in:  ui/components/game_button.gd (GameButton.Kind.MENU): every action
            button: Host a game, Join a game, the two offline matches,
            Ready, Leave, Back to lobby, Copy code, Scan, Cancel, ...
            styles "Button" in ui/theme/game_theme.gd
  size:     384x96 nine-patch per state, 24 px margins; shown at least
            300x56 px. States: normal, hover, pressed (also a toggled Ready),
            hover_pressed, disabled, focus (a frame only, drawn on top of the
            others: transparent middle).
  style:    a worn wooden plank or iron-banded leather tag; pressed sinks in
            and warms to burnt sienna; disabled is darker and duller, not
            greyed out to blue.
  covered:  #70

ui_choice_button  The wide toggle button of the perk choice, in six states.
  used in:  ui/components/game_button.gd (GameButton.Kind.CHOICE) in
            ui/lobby/perk_picker.gd -> PerkPicker/Perk_<id>;
            style ChoiceButton in ui/theme/game_theme.gd
  size:     512x128 nine-patch per state, 24 px margins; shown 460 px wide and

  style:    a parchment card or a slip of paper pinned to the board.
  covered:  #70

perk_icon_<id>  One icon per perk, plus one for "no perk".
  ids:      wide_flashlight, long_flashlight, faster_reload, shepherd_dog,

            allied_bat (Monster); none
  used in:  PerkDefinition.icon in systems/perks/definitions/<id>.tres (empty
            today), shown by ui/lobby/perk_picker.gd through
            GameButton.set_choice_icon (a 44x44 slot) and, for the active
            perks, as the HUD's Perk icon (76x76) and the Perk touch button
            (108x108)
  size:     128x128, transparent
  style:    one simple, high-contrast object per perk (a lantern with a wide
            beam, a long beam, a cartridge, a dog's head, a hidden bullet,
            gnashing teeth, a fading silhouette, a bat), painted but with a
            clear silhouette; must read at 44 px. "none": an empty hook or
            a crossed-out circle.
  covered:  #70

sprite uses a texture outside such a folder. Sizes, prompts and the exact
look are in #30; only what #30 does not cover is described here.
