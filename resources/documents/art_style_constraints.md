# Art Style Constraints: Atmospheric Realism Painting

## Core Aesthetic Definition
The visual style is strictly defined as "Atmospheric Realism Painting." All imagery must look as though it was created using traditional, physical painting mediums (such as oil or heavy acrylic on canvas) by a master of realism. The tone is grim, tense, and grounded in survival horror, heavily emphasizing isolation and decay. Proportions of characters, animals, and environments must remain strictly realistic and anatomically correct. To enforce the spatial layout, the environment must exclusively be rendered from a strict, unyielding top-down perspective, ensuring the ground plane fills the entire canvas without any horizon or lateral depth.

## Strict Camera and Perspective Lock (CRITICAL)
*   **Camera Angle:** Strict 90-degree zenithal camera, orthographic overhead view looking directly down at the ground.
*   **Subject Visibility:** Characters and objects must be seen directly from above, showing only the tops of heads, shoulders, and the uppermost surfaces of environmental props.
*   **Environment:** The flat horizontal floor plane must fill 100% of the frame from edge to edge. 
*   **Prohibition on Angles:** Zero camera tilt, zero horizon, and absolutely no side-profiles of walls, bodies, or structures.

## Texture and Medium Execution
*   **Medium:** Traditional oil painting realism.
*   **Brushwork:** Visible, textured brushstrokes. Use impasto (thickly applied paint) for illuminated areas and highlights, and thin, dark washes for deeply shadowed areas.
*   **Canvas:** A subtle physical canvas or wood panel texture should be perceivable beneath the paint.
*   **Edge Control:** Edges should be painted and blended naturally. Hard edges should exist only where light sharply hits an object; otherwise, forms should melt softly into the surrounding darkness.

## Lighting and Shading (Chiaroscuro)
*   **Contrast:** Extreme, stark contrast between light and shadow (Tenebrism).
*   **Illumination:** Lighting must appear highly directional and artificial (e.g., a harsh flashlight or torch cone) cutting through an overarching, oppressive darkness.
*   **Shadows:** Shadows must be deep, heavy, and pitch-black or dark umber. Unlit areas should consume the details of the environment, leaving only faint, suggestive brushstrokes of shapes lurking in the dark. Shadows must stretch radially and flatly across the ground plane to firmly anchor the top-down perspective and prevent the illusion of 3D depth.

## Color Palette
*   **Base Tones:** Muted, desaturated, and earthy. Focus on raw umber, burnt sienna, olive greens, muddy browns, and charcoal blacks.
*   **Highlights:** Pale, sickly yellows or harsh, cold whites for areas hit directly by the light source.
*   **Saturation:** Strictly low saturation. Avoid any vibrant, neon, or overly pure hues. Blood (if present) should be a dark, oxidized crimson or rust color, not bright red.

## Positive Prompt Injection Keywords (Mandatory Inclusions)
*   Strict 90-degree zenithal camera angle, flat ground filling 100% of frame, seen directly from above
*   Traditional oil painting on canvas
*   Realistic proportions and anatomy
*   Visible, textured brushstrokes
*   Heavy chiaroscuro, tenebrism
*   Grim, macabre, atmospheric survival horror
*   Harsh directional lighting against pitch-black shadows
*   Muted, earthy color palette, darkwood tones

## Negative Constraints (Strictly Avoid)
*   NO 3/4 view, NO isometric perspective, NO angled camera, NO horizon lines, NO tilt-shift
*   NO digital smoothing, airbrushing, or pristine digital blending
*   NO 3D rendering, CGI, Octane Render, or Unreal Engine aesthetics
*   NO cell-shading, comic book outlines, or ink lines (avoid *Darkest Dungeon* line-art styles; lean entirely into realism)
*   NO anime, manga, cartoon, or stylized caricatures
*   NO flat vectors or flat colors
*   NO bright, saturated, or cheerful colors
*   NO modern sci-fi or polished fantasy elements
*   NO photographic hyper-realism (it must look like a *painting*, not a photograph)
