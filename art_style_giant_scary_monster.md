# Image Generation Specification: "Giant Scary Monster"

This document outlines the precise prompt elements and stylistic constraints required to generate the Phase 2 "Giant Scary Monster" from *Hide in Sheep Clothing*.

## 1. Core Concept & Perspective
*   **Perspective:** Strictly 2D top-down view (bird's-eye view, 90 degrees looking straight down).
*   **Subject:** A horrifying biological anomaly where a regular sheep body is the central anchor for a massive, terrifying arachnid-like creature.

## 2. Subject Anatomy & Composition
*   **Central Body:** A realistic, anatomically correct sheep body viewed directly from above. The wool is visible, illuminated against the dark ground.
*   **Appendages (Legs):** 
    *   **Count:** Exactly 20 legs.
    *   **Arrangement:** 10 legs sprouting from the left side of the sheep body, 10 legs sprouting from the right side.
    *   **Texture & Appearance:** The legs are entirely hairless, smooth, and glossy black, resembling enormous spider or crustacean limbs with segmented joints.
    *   **Scale:** The legs are monstrously huge, dwarfing the central sheep body and explosively sprawling outward to dominate the entire canvas.

## 3. Art Style & Medium (Atmospheric Realism)
*   **Medium:** Must look exactly like a traditional oil painting on a physical canvas.
*   **Brushwork:** Visible, textured brushstrokes. Use thick impasto techniques for the illuminated wool of the sheep and the highlights on the glossy black legs.
*   **Aesthetic:** Grim, macabre, and grounded in survival horror. No digital smoothing, no cartoon outlines, and no 3D render aesthetics.

## 4. Lighting, Shadow & Environment
*   **Chiaroscuro & Tenebrism:** Extreme contrast between light and dark.
*   **Lighting Source:** Harsh, directional, artificial lighting focused on the center (the sheep body and the inner joints of the legs).
*   **Shadows:** The edges of the canvas and the gaps between the massive legs must melt softly into deep, heavy, pitch-black shadows.
*   **Environment:** The ground beneath should barely be visible, suggested only by muted, earthy brushstrokes of dirt and dead grass fading into the dark.

## 5. Color Palette
*   **Base:** Muted, low-saturation, earthy tones (raw umber, muddy brown, charcoal black).
*   **Highlights:** Pale, sickly yellows and harsh whites where the light strikes the sheep's back and the joints of the black legs.

## 6. Negative Prompting (What to strictly avoid)
*   NO hair or fur on the spider legs (must be smooth).
*   NO side-angles, isometric, or 3D perspectives (must be strictly flat top-down).
*   NO digital airbrushing, cell-shading, or vector graphics.
*   NO vibrant, neon, or cheerful colors.
